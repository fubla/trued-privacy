Requesting deletion of your data

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data collected by Trued – field ballistics, published by Heimo. It is part of the Trued privacy policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Email contact@heimo.ai with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Send it from the same email address you used to contact us. If you cannot, describe the report instead — roughly when you sent it, and what it said. Trued has no user accounts, so this is how a request is matched to a report.
  3. You will get a reply confirming the deletion, normally within a few days and at the latest within 30 days of the request.

There is no charge, and you do not need an account or an app installation to ask.

Trued has no user accounts, so there is nothing to delete beyond the crash reports and feedback above. Uninstalling the app removes what Trued itself stores on your device. It does not remove cards you exported into your photo gallery or sent to another app — those left Trued when you saved or shared them, and only you can delete them.

If you would rather not send anything at all

Switching off Settings → Send crash reports and feedback stops Trued collecting anything new. One narrow exception: a report captured before you switched it off, but not yet delivered because the device was offline, can still be sent the next time the app starts.

Turning on Mission Mode stops all reporting regardless of that setting — automatic crash reports included, and any already-captured report still waiting to be sent, because it prevents the reporting library from starting at all.
