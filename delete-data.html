Requesting deletion of your data

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data collected by Trued – field ballistics, published by Heimo. It is part of the Trued privacy policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Email contact@heimo.ai with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Send it from the same email address you used to contact us. If you cannot, describe the report instead — roughly when you sent it, and what it said. Trued has no user accounts, so this is how a request is matched to a report.
  3. You will get a reply confirming the deletion, normally within a few days and at the latest within 30 days of the request.

There is no charge, and you do not need an account or an app installation to ask.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhere it isDeleted on requestRetention
Crash reports — app version and build number, device platform and OS version, language setting, and the technical stack trace Sentry, hosted in the European Union Yes Automatically deleted 30 days after it is received, whether or not you ask
Feedback you wrote in the app — the text you typed, with the same five tags Sentry, hosted in the European Union Yes Automatically deleted 30 days after it is received, whether or not you ask
Your rifle profiles, bullet data, environmental inputs, DOPE corrections and exported cards Your device only — never uploaded, and never included in a crash report or feedback Not applicable — we never receive it Kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app
Account details, name, email contact list, advertising identifiers, location Not collected at all Nothing to delete None

Trued has no user accounts, so there is nothing to delete beyond the crash reports and feedback above. Uninstalling the app removes everything held on the device.

If you would rather not send anything at all

Crash reporting and feedback are off-limits to us the moment you switch them off: Settings → Send crash reports and feedback. Turning on Mission Mode stops all reporting regardless of that setting, including automatic crash reports.
