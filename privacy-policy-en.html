Trued — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026 · Suomeksi

Trued collects no personal data, sends no telemetry, and makes no network connections. Trued is a ballistic calculator. Everything it computes and stores stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Trued has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The app declares no INTERNET permission and makes zero network calls — it cannot transmit data even in principle.

Data stored on your device

Your rifle profiles, bullet data, environmental inputs, DOPE corrections, and exported cards are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes them.

Location

If you enable it, Trued reads your device GPS to obtain latitude (for the Coriolis effect) and altitude (for air density). This happens entirely on the device, is used only for the ballistic solution, is gated by the app's EMCON (emissions-control) mode, and is never stored off-device or transmitted. The app is fully usable without location by entering values manually.

Sensors

Where available and permitted, Trued reads the barometer and compass on the device for environmental inputs. These readings are used on-device only and are not transmitted.

Children

Trued is intended for adult precision shooters and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds optional network features (for example profile sync), this policy will be updated and the change disclosed before that feature is enabled.
